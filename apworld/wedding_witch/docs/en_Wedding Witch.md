# Wedding Witch custom rules

73 passive rank items, 2 difficulty items, 5 EXP unlocks, 62 progressive skill ranks in 13 types. One EXP type starts unlocked. Masteries remain vanilla.

142 checks: 18 map clears, 6 full transformations, transformEnd ending milestones and 118-transformEnd cumulative flower pickups split by difficulty. Goal: transformEnd distinct successful endings on the selected difficulty. Normal/untransformed is one of seven endings.

Received standard skill ranks become permanently owned and automatically active; they are applied again at the start of each run and excluded from level-up choices. Potion offers retain strength and quantity. Permanent passives replace native purchases and take effect on the next run.
