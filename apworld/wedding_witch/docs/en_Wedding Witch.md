# Wedding Witch custom rules

80 items: 73 passive rank items, 2 difficulty items and 5 EXP unlocks. One EXP type starts unlocked. The 62 Skill Unlock items are removed; all 13 standard skills use native level-up choices for the current run. Masteries remain vanilla.

80 checks: 18 map clears, 6 full transformations, 38 native achievements, transformEnd ending milestones and 18-transformEnd cumulative flower pickups split by difficulty. Removing the 62 skill items also removes 62 flower checks. Goal: transformEnd distinct successful endings on the selected difficulty. Normal/untransformed is one of seven endings.

Standard skills can be selected and upgraded at level-up, with native caps and no ranks carried to the next run. Potion offers retain strength and quantity. Permanent passives replace native purchases and take effect on the next run.

The native starter-spell selection is retained alongside standard skills. Higher potion conditions and the equipped-spell limit still apply. Achievement checks use the native conditions and this seed's counters, and are sent automatically on completion. A six-colour AP charm replaces the gold icon and amount; these achievements give no coins. Natural flower pickups continue counting toward the Florist achievement after the AP flower-check budget is exhausted.
